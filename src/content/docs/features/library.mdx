## Adding a recipe

A recipe has a title (the only required field), an optional hero photo,
description, servings, prep/cook time, difficulty (easy/medium/hard),
source, cuisine, tags, and a video link, plus a list of ingredients
(quantity, unit, name, note) and steps (text, with an optional photo per
step). Uploaded photos are capped by the server's image size limit — see
[Settings](/api/settings). Source, cuisine, tags, and the video link live
under **Additional details** in the recipe form, collapsed by default.

## Video

Paste a link — a YouTube video embeds right in the recipe's detail view;
any other link shows as a "Watch the video" button instead. It's an
optional field you can add, change, or clear at any time from Edit, so a
recipe you typed in by hand today can get a video attached later. A
recipe with a video shows a small play icon on its thumbnail throughout
the library. Importing directly from a YouTube URL sets this
automatically — see [Import from a URL](/features/import).

## Cooking

- The servings control scales every ingredient quantity up or down —
  whole numbers, simple fractions ("1/2"), and mixed numbers ("1 1/2")
  scale cleanly; anything else is left as-is rather than mangled
- Turn on **Settings → Library → Ingredient gram weights** (admin only)
  to show an approximate gram weight next to convertible quantities, e.g.
  "1 cup flour (~120g)" — calculated offline from a built-in density
  table for common baking ingredients, so it's not exact for everything
- **Cook Mode** — open a recipe and hit the chef's-hat button for a
  full-screen, one-step-at-a-time view. Move between steps with the
  on-screen arrows, a swipe, or the ← / → keys; the ingredient list is
  one tap away, and the screen stays awake on browsers that support the
  Wake Lock API so it doesn't dim mid-recipe
- Mark a recipe **cooked** to stamp it with today's date and surface it
  under Recently Cooked

## Organizing and finding a recipe

- Rate any recipe 1–5 stars, or favorite it
- Search by title from the search dialog
- **Filter** the Library, Favorites, or a collection by tag, cuisine, or
  difficulty from the Filters button next to Sort — picking more than one
  tag (or cuisine, or difficulty) broadens the results within that facet,
  while combining tag + cuisine + difficulty narrows across facets. Your
  filter choices are remembered per page
- On a shared install, a recipe's detail panel shows **Cooking Activity** —
  which other profiles have cooked, rated, or favorited it
- **Settings → Stats** shows a quick overview of the install — recipe,
  collection, and profile counts, plus your own favorited, cooked, and
  average rating numbers

Planning a grocery run? Add recipes to your
[shopping list](/features/shopping-list) and get one consolidated,
checkable ingredient list.

## Removing a recipe

Deleting a recipe is admin-only — see [Profiles & Access](/features/profiles).
